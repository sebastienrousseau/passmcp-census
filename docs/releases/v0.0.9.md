## Highlights ⭐️

* **In lockstep with passmcp 0.0.9**: passmcp-census moves from 0.0.7 to the family's version. No census edition is published in this release.
* **The census runs passmcp v0.0.9**: the flags the census passes and the report fields it reads are unchanged; `protocol.unknown_resource` (SEP-2164) adds a missing-resource check when `capabilities.resources` is declared, capability gating skips `protocol.invalid_params` and `protocol.unknown_tool` when `capabilities.tools` is undeclared, and `catalog.cache_hints` checks SEP-2549 hints across declared list endpoints on `2026-07-28`.
* **Manual and CI dependency hygiene**: the manual's hash-locked build tree moves to `mkdocs-material` 9.8.0, `pymdown-extensions` 12.3, and `certifi` 2026.10.23, and CI workflows pin `github/codeql-action` 4.39.3.
